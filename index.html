Minecraft: Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Creative freedom to build anything.
  • Educational value: resource management and problem solving.
  • Massive community with mods and servers.

Cons

  • Can be addictive and time-consuming.
  • Graphics are blocky and may not appeal to everyone.
  • Requires updates and sometimes paid add-ons.
